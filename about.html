SSOA Heritage

Nurturing Leaders Since 2007

Sahjanand School of Achievers was established under the guiding light of our dedicated trustees. Starting with a handful of pupils, we have grown into a premier education standard with dual campuses hosting 2100+ students and 140+ expert teachers.

Our Vision

To maintain the top position in the field of education and to become a premier school of achievers recognized for its care, discipline and ultimate teaching.

Our Mission

The mission of Sahjanand School of Achievers is to provide the best in education for preparing students for a diverse, highly technological, information-based global society. The school encourages the students to build their career.

Educational Creed

Our Philosophy

"We respect the dignity and worth of all the students under our care. We believe that all students can be nurtured to achieve their full potential within a school environment that is both caring and challenging. We expect all students to be respectful and responsible and to rise to the challenges of life."

Educational Creed

Our Core Beliefs

Our mentors, trustees, faculties and staff commit to provide a happy, caring and stimulating educational environment where the learners recognize and achieve their fullest potential.

Parental Care

We care and are committed to exercise personal care of each student to help them live their dreams.

Method to Achieve

We believe in brief teaching, service and technology. We are committed to promoting creative and critical thinking among faculty, students and staff within a culture of inspiration, high expectations, accountability and quality service to students.

Communication and Collaboration

We value open communication with parents to find the way to success in their and their ward's dreams, and we treasure the valuable trust placed in us by parents.
