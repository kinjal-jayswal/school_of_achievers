Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school timings for both campuses?
Both the Chiloda Campus (KG - Standard 10) and Sargasan Campus (Class 11 - 12 Science stream) are open from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
Is school transport available for students?
Yes, we operate a fleet of safe, supervisor-monitored school buses covering most major sectors of Gandhinagar, Chiloda, and surrounding rural routes. Detailed routes can be requested at the reception desk.
What board is the school affiliated with?
Sahjanand School of Achievers is fully affiliated with the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (G.S.E.B.) for both Secondary and Higher Secondary streams.
